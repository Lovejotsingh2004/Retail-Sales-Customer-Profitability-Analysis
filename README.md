# Retail Sales & Customer Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, customer performance, product performance, and the impact of discounts on profit.

The analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel, MySQL, and Tableau.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales and profit.
- Identify the most profitable customers.
- Identify products affected by high discounts and low profitability.
- Analyze monthly profit trends.
- Compare profitability across product sub-categories.
- Analyze the relationship between sales and profit.
- Generate useful business insights and recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- MySQL
- Tableau Public
- CSV Dataset

## Dataset

The project uses the Superstore retail dataset containing order, customer, product, sales, discount, quantity, and profit information.

The dataset contains 10,195 records and 21 columns.

## Data Cleaning

Data preparation was performed in Microsoft Excel.

The main cleaning steps included:

- Creating a working copy of the dataset
- Converting the data into a table
- Checking the dataset structure
- Removing duplicate records
- Checking missing values
- Correcting data types
- Formatting Order Date and Ship Date
- Formatting numerical fields such as Sales, Quantity, Discount and Profit

## SQL Analysis

MySQL was used to perform analytical queries on the retail sales data.

The SQL analysis focused on:

- Sales and profit analysis
- Customer profitability
- Product performance
- Discount analysis
- Sub-category profitability
- Business performance analysis

## Tableau Analysis

The cleaned dataset was imported into Tableau Public to create interactive visualizations and dashboards.

### Key Visualizations

1. **Top 10 Profitable Customers**
   - Identifies customers generating the highest profit.

2. **High Discount – Low Profit Products**
   - Highlights products where high discounts are associated with low or negative profit.

3. **Monthly Profit Trend**
   - Shows how profit changes over time.

4. **Profit by Sub-Category**
   - Compares profitability across different product sub-categories.

5. **Sales vs Profit by Sub-Category**
   - Compares sales volume with the profit generated by each sub-category.

## Key Insights

- A small group of customers contributes significantly to overall profit.
- Higher discounts can negatively affect profitability.
- Some products generate losses despite having sales.
- Profitability varies significantly across product sub-categories.
- Monthly profit changes over time and shows periods of stronger and weaker performance.
- High sales do not always result in high profit.

## Business Recommendations

- Avoid excessive discounts on products with low profit margins.
- Focus on retaining high-profit customers.
- Review products that consistently generate losses.
- Analyze sub-categories with strong profitability and prioritize them.
- Use sales and profit together when evaluating business performance rather than relying only on sales.
